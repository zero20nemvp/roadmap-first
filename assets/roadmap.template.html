The roadmap for

THE JOB THAT PAYS
Ex 1–3 The wall

Ex 3 + 5 The journey

The A3 strip, one step per box, with the feature that makes each step possible stuck underneath. A pink note is a step stage 1 can’t do yet.

Ex 4–6 Size it in pieces

A piece is one thing to build or switch on: a screen, a prompt, a kind of data, an outside service. Shared pieces count once, so every cut has an exact price.

Ex 6 Argue it down

Would they still pay without it?

Every stage-1 feature was asked this. A yes moves it out. The founder has the last word.

Ex 4 + 6 The flipchart

Stage 1 is what gets sold first. Nothing in a stage needs a stage after it.

Ex 7 Stage 1’s stories

One story per step, each with “done when”

Stories come last, so none get written for features that were cut. On build day each one is one thing to build, and its “done when” is the check.

**Subject:** Six steps from a problem to a release

Hi {{name}},

The workshop ran one method, and you can run it again on your own:

1. **Problems.** What's annoying, slow or expensive for your customer right now?
2. **Workarounds.** What do they do about it today? This is the job in disguise. If they already pay for a workaround, your first release is billable.
3. **Jobs.** So what are they really trying to get done?
4. **Features.** What does your product need to do that job?
5. **Stories.** What does each feature need, in small user stories you can check off?
6. **Releases.** When do you ship, and which stories go in each? The first one is Ground Zero.

Jobs stay the linchpin: jobs give features, and features give stories. You just don't start from them, because nobody can name a job cold. Everybody can name a problem.

One rule holds it together: **every release completes at least one job end to end.** That keeps each release usable, and something you can charge for.

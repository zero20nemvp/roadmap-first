# Stuck or lost

## “I don't know”

Say it, in any words: “I don't know”, “not sure”, “no idea”, “I'm stuck”. The coach never answers for you, but it won't leave you stuck either. Each time you say it on the same question, it climbs one rung:

1. **It asks again, more simply,** in plainer words, from your customer's side.
2. **It shows how Redline answered it,** in one line from the workshop's example, then asks for yours.
3. **It offers two or three options.** You pick one, change it, or reject them all. It never picks for you.
4. **It offers to park it.** The question moves to your **unknowns to test**, and you carry on.

You can park at any time without climbing the ladder: say “park it”, or run `/021 park`.

Parked questions aren't forgotten. Before the brief, you'll be asked how you'd test each one, because an assumption with no test stays a guess.

## “Where am I?”

Type it, or “I'm lost”, “what's next” or “help”, and before anything else the coach tells you in three lines where you are, what's done and what comes next. For the whole route, use `/021 where`. Or glance at the band above the prompt: it always shows the exercise, your progress and the next question.

## “Why are you asking this?”

`/021 why` shows the open question, why it matters, and what “done” means for this exercise.

## “I want to change something I said”

Use `/021 back <n>` to go back to exercise `n`. You can also just say what you want to change, like “actually the paying job is J3”, and the coach will record it. If the change opens new gaps, you'll be asked about them. Nothing else is lost.

## “This is taking too long”

- Stop whenever you like. Everything is saved after every answer, and `/021 roadmap` picks up where you left off.
- Short answers are fine. You can sharpen them later.
- Park what you can't answer yet.
- Check the band: `~N min left` is honest about what's ahead.

## “I disagree with the coach”

Say so. You have the last word on everything that matters: who pays, the jobs, the price, what to keep or cut, and what done looks like. At exercise 6 Claude argues against your features on purpose, and it pushes back once. After that, what you say is recorded with your reason.

## Starting over

Move or rename the `021` folder, then run `/021 roadmap`:

```
mv 021 021-first-try
```

**Subject:** Price each release, and keep your first customers

Hi {{name}},

Releases give you a pricing tool for free: **grandfather pricing**.

Whoever joins at Ground Zero keeps the Ground Zero price forever. When R1 ships, new customers pay the R1 price, and Ground Zero customers still pay theirs. Cancel, and the old price is gone. Early customers get a reason to start now, and a reason to stay.

It works for other kinds of product too:

- **One-off purchase.** Sell Ground Zero at a launch price and include every later release. Buyers at R2 pay the R2 price.
- **Marketplace.** Lock the commission rate for sellers who join early. Each release that adds value for sellers raises the rate for new ones only.
- **Usage-based.** Lock the per-unit rate for early accounts. Later releases change the rate for new accounts, not existing ones.

Have a look at your flight plan and write a price next to each waypoint: [your report]({{report_link}})
